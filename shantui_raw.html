Excavator
Shantui presently boasts nearly 30 years of production history since the manufacturing of first excavator in 1994. At present, Shantui excavator product lineup covers >30 product models in 1.2t~200t and meets the demands of >95% customers. Relying on the resource advantages of Shandong Heavy Industry Group, Shantui is the domestic sole excavator brand with proprietary power system, hydraulic system, and chassis.
